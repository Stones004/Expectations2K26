E [X]PECTATIONS is the annual intercollegiate fest organized by the Department of Statistics and Data Science at CHRIST (Deemed to be University).

This year's theme The Odyssey - Navigating the Seas of Data.

Join us for an exciting array of technical and non-technical events that challenge your analytical skills, creativity, and teamwork. Whether you're a data enthusiast or just curious about the world of statistics, E[X]PECTATIONS'26 has something for everyone.

Accommodation

We are pleased to offer accommodation for students participating from colleges outside Bangalore. Please note that the accommodation is limited to students and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

27th & 28th September Limited Availability


The accommodation will be available on 27 and 28 September (3 days , 2 Nights)

Registration
